## Aspect checks

South Morning, North Night, No.15, Shop, Fish Book, Area Map, No.00 fight/final choice and Contact journal also have `_19_5x9`, `_20x9`, `_640x360` variants in this folder. Native 16:9 captures are 1280×720; the low-resolution render is 640×360. Checkpoints A–E and earlier refinement renders are kept in [checkpoints](checkpoints/).

## Reviewing

Judge lake beauty, boat/fish readability, HUD hierarchy, night/depth separation and commercial feel on a phone. Functional/layout PASS is not artistic approval. No.00 should read as an unbounded terrain-like partial form, with no head/eyes/mouth.
